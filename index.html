📱 How Offline Mobile + Laptop Sync Works

Step 1: Install on Mobile (One-Time)

Open this page once in your mobile's Chrome or Safari browser. Tap the 3 dots menu and select "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".

Step 2: When Laptop is OFF

The app opens from your phone's memory automatically! You can search items, check kg/gram prices, and record sales without any network or server. Everything saves securely in your phone.

Step 3: When Laptop Turns ON

Turn on your laptop and start start_mobile_server.bat. Tap "Sync with Laptop" on your phone. All sales done on your phone will automatically upload to your laptop, and any new items or rates from your laptop will download to your phone!
