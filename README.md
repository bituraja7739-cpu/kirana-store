# 🛒 KiranaPro - Grocery & Kirana Store Stock & Rate Management Software (v3.2 Live Cloud Sync)

A fast, lightweight, and offline-capable stock, rate, and POS billing management application built specifically for grocery and retail stores with real-time cloud auto-sync between mobile and laptop.

---

## 🌟 Key Features:

1. **⚡ Instant Search & Direct Rate Display:**
   - Search by item name or category.
   - Instantly shows **Purchase Rate (Buy Price)**, **Selling Rate**, **Profit Margin**, and **Current Available Stock**.

2. **✏️ Dedicated Quick Rate Edit Option:**
   - Click the **"✏️ Edit Rates"** button on any item card or table row.
   - A quick modal pops up to edit both **Purchase Rate** and **Selling Rate** in 2 seconds without navigating away.
   - Automatically recalculates your profit margins and gram/kg breakdown.

3. **⚖️ Comprehensive Unit Conversions (kg / gram / pcs / litre / ml):**
   - **Gram Breakdown:** Shows rates for 50g, 100g, 250g (1/4 kg), 500g (1/2 kg), 1kg, 2kg, 5kg automatically.
   - **Reverse Rupee Calculator:** If a customer asks *"Give me ₹20 or ₹50 worth of sugar"*, simply enter the rupee amount and it calculates the exact grams to weigh out.
   - **Pieces & Packets:** Full support for count-based items (soaps, biscuits, packets, bottles).

4. **🛒 Sales Entry & POS Billing:**
   - Add items to the customer cart with unit selection (kg, gm, pcs, litre, ml).
   - Enter discount and select payment mode: **Cash, UPI / Online, or Credit (Khata)**.
   - **Stock Auto-deduction:** Deducts sold quantity from inventory automatically.
   - **Receipt Slip Printing:** 1-click thermal or standard paper receipt printing.

5. **📦 Inward Stock Management (+ Add Stock):**
   - Add newly arrived stock quantity and optionally update purchase/selling rates when wholesale costs change.

6. **📋 Sales History & Excel Export:**
   - Live dashboard counter for **Today's Total Sales**.
   - View past bills and export your entire sales record to an Excel spreadsheet (`.xlsx`).

7. **🔒 100% Offline, Fast & Private:**
   - No internet required.
   - Safe local storage with one-click **JSON Backup & Restore**.

---

## 🚀 How to Run:

- Double-click **`start_app.bat`** (or open **`index.html`** in any web browser like Chrome or Edge).
- To access from a mobile phone in the shop, double-click **`start_mobile_server.bat`** and open the displayed IP address on your phone.
